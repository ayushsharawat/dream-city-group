FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about plot allotment, legal documentation, car giveaway rules, and township development at Dream Residency.

Where is Dream Residency located and what is its road connectivity?
Dream Residency is situated at a prime strategic corridor in Sector 1, Shamli, Uttar Pradesh. It features prompt arterial connectivity to the Shamli Bypass, Delhi-Saharanpur Highway, central commercial zones, and Shamli Railway Station (within 10-12 minutes).
How does the Hyundai Creta & Mahindra XUV 7XO giveaway draw work?
To celebrate our Grand Opening on 25 October 2026, Dream City Group is holding a transparent celebration draw for registered plot allottees. Plot No. 121 qualifies for the Hyundai Creta SUV and Plot No. 201 qualifies for the Mahindra XUV 7XO SUV. All allotment draw proceedings follow official documented criteria.
Are the plots freehold with clear title registry and mutation?
Yes, 100%. All plots at Dream Residency and throughout Dream City Group projects carry clear, marketable, and encumbrance-free freehold titles. Our legal office assists allottees directly with registry and revenue mutation procedures.
What road widths and physical infrastructure are provided on-site?
The township features heavy-duty 40ft and 30ft wide asphalt roads, underground drainage pipeline systems, overhead sweet water supply network, LED street illumination, grand gated entrance with security boom barriers, CCTV surveillance, and landscaped family parks with a temple.
Can I schedule a free guided site inspection with the developer?
Absolutely! Our Shamli sales desk offers daily guided on-site inspections. You can call directly at +91 82185 53028 or tap our WhatsApp link to arrange a site visit at your convenience.
